Nella is your money companion — and she lives in your chat.

Instead of hunting through menus, you say what you need. Tell Nella you want to send money, buy airtime, pay a bill or check your balance, and she takes you through it one step at a time. Every payment is quoted before you approve it: recipient, amount, fee and total, in one clear screen.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Send money to any Nigerian bank account, with the recipient's name checked before you pay
• Buy airtime and data for yourself or someone else
• Pay electricity bills, DStv, GOtv and Startimes
• Check your balance and open a receipt for any payment
• Keep a list of the people you have paid, with their account details
• Approve payments with your PIN in a private sheet, never in the chat
• Reply, react, and send a voice note or a photo in the conversation itself
• Switch between light, dark or your phone's setting

HOW IT WORKS
Your Nella account is a real Nigerian wallet, opened after identity checks and held with VFD Microfinance Bank. Fund it with a transfer from any bank, then spend from it in the chat. Your money is held by the bank, not by Nella.

WHAT IT COSTS
Every quote shows the fee before you approve. Many transfers are free, and the amount you approve is the amount that executes.

SAFETY
Payments need your 4-digit PIN, entered only in the secure approval sheet. Nella never asks for your PIN, OTP or bank password in a chat, an email or a phone call. A first payment to a new recipient can be held briefly for your protection — we tell you when that happens.
