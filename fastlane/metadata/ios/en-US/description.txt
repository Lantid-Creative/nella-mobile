Nella is your money companion — and she lives in your chat.

Instead of hunting through menus, you say what you need. Tell Nella you want to send money, buy airtime or pay a bill, and she takes you through it one step at a time, showing exactly what will happen before anything is submitted.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Start a transfer and see a full breakdown before you approve anything
• Buy airtime or data by chatting
• Keep the conversation next to your payment details, so nothing gets lost
• Reply, react, and send a voice note or a photo in the chat itself
• Switch between light, dark or your phone's setting

THIS RELEASE IS A PREVIEW
This version is a hands-on preview. It runs entirely on your device: no bank account is connected, no real money moves, and nothing you type leaves your phone. Live banking, verified accounts and real payments arrive in a future update.

WHY CHAT?
Money is easier to talk about than to click through. Nella asks one clear question at a time, repeats the details back to you and never hides a fee — like messaging a helpful friend who happens to be very careful with your money.

We are building in the open. Tell us what feels slow, confusing or missing: the chat is the product, and it gets better with every conversation.
